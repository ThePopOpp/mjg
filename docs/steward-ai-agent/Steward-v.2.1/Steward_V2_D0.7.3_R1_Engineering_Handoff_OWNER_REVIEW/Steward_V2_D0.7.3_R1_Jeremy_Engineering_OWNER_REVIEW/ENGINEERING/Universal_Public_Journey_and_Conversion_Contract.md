# D0.7.3 — Universal public journey, attribution and conversion engineering contract

## J-UNI-01 One application, one assessment, one Free experience
MichaelJGauthier.com provides Created for More (preferred) and direct Steward registration without the assessment (eligible users). Campaign URLs/QR codes (including Compass) land on the SAME instrument/version, scoring engine, results experience, AI source-serving policy, Free access level, Blueprint page architecture, and published offer/upgrade flow. If verified inputs and actual entitlements match, generated guidance must be equivalent in substance; wording randomness is not a parity failure. The source code may affect aggregate attribution but **never** modify AI prompts, recommendation policy, assessment scoring, pricing eligibility, church affiliations, grants or third-party sharing. A visitor can skip optional church fields and ForgedLife participation, and decline purchasing, without losing ordinary Free value. Never require assessment for direct-entry Free participants.

## J-UNI-02 Results handoff and consent
Deterministic service computes exact CFM4 seven-layer scores from actual responses; the separate Energy Audit uses its own four energies and scoring. Preserve instrument version, dates, missingness and real participant/account binding. Assessment-first result view appears before mandatory account signup when approved; save/continuation uses verified identity, existing account recovery/MFA and explicitly required consents. Results email deep links to the same authenticated Steward interpretation, not a private-data-bearing URL. AG07 interprets scores, strengths, concerns and patterns with tentative language, sufficient teaching depth and a choice: explore a strength, examine a concern, start a Free Blueprint. No invented item ratings, fake complete scores, low-score sales trigger or automatic Blueprint approval.

## J-UNI-03 Verified Full/Premium progression
Present the same applicable published public offer to equally eligible participants, regardless of attribution. Only confirmed checkout/payment + committed pending entitlement + explicit activation and server receipt can establish active Premium for the **new** $299 initial Full+12-month Premium offer with **separately accepted $199 annual renewal after explicit activation**, distinct from the legacy $299 nonrenewing prepaid offer. Preserve $149 one-time option, eligible $19 monthly / $199 annual continuation, valid existing snapshots and no-card grants. Historical Compass 60-day / leader 12-month configurations remain recorded but are NEVER automatically issued just because a referral source or campaign is Compass. Any authorized separate sponsor grant has its own identity, scope, consent and nonbilling behavior. Campaign codes are not grant codes.

## J-UNI-04 Minimal measurement contract
Use allowlisted operational events with server-side participant pseudonymous ID where lawful/consented, event_version, canonical event time UTC, dedupe/idempotency key, instrument/version when necessary, attribution_source with provenance and unknown value, consent-policy/version and limited journey stage. No raw answers, free-form prompts, conversations, private Blueprint text, faith statements, journals, inferred motives, church affiliation or other sensitive content in the conversion/marketing event stream. Identity and attribution joins remain purpose-limited and access-controlled. Do not create person-level tracking without appropriate legal/consent/retention basis. If identity does not link across anonymous→verified, report separate anonymous event counts rather than invent a person-level funnel. No fabricated missing stages.

### Allowed funnel events and truth definitions
| Event | Verified fact required |
|---|---|
| `assessment_started` | form actually initialized; report anonymous/verified denominators separately |
| `assessment_completed` | deterministic scoring accepted eligible complete instrument (or explicitly labeled partial under instrument rules) |
| `free_steward_first_conversation` | actual eligible first Free assistant response for a verified participant, whether assessment-linked **or direct-entry without assessment**; record `assessment_origin` (`cfm4`, `direct_entry_no_assessment`, or `unknown`) separately and do not invent scores |
| `free_meaningful_engagement` | reviewed non-content operational criterion such as a separate participant-initiated return conversation or explicitly approved Free Blueprint action; define threshold before reporting |
| `full_purchase_initiated` / `premium_purchase_initiated` | authenticated valid checkout started, never counted as paid conversion |
| `purchase_confirmed` | verified payment and associated purchase order reconciled, deduplicated; not browser return or click |
| `premium_activated` | explicit participant action with server-confirmed activation (distinct from purchase) |
| `post_conversion_engagement` | qualifying observed use after effective paid service, according to defined non-content engagement rule |
| `voluntary_usefulness_feedback` | explicit participant response to optional feedback, not model-inferred transformation |

### 30-day Free-to-Premium conversion cohort
**Primary denominator:** distinct verified participants whose **first eligible Free Steward conversation** occurred in the cohort period, without an active Premium entitlement at that timestamp; report assessment-origin and direct-entry subgroups separately. **Numerator:** denominator participants with first verified Premium-purchase completion within **30 × 24 hours** after that Free entry timestamp and before exclusive window end. Report event timestamp basis UTC, report-as-of time, eligibility exclusions, observed/matured cohorts separately, and unique-person deduplication. Purchases must be provider-verified/committed; checkout starts not counted. The rate is numerator / eligible matured cohort denominator. If a participant entered Free under a no-card benefit or is not purchase-eligible, disclose and consistently handle exclusions rather than invent conversion potential; specify the cohort in dashboard metadata. Also report Full purchases separately from Premium purchase and separately from activation, with conversion-to-Premium defined by verified Premium purchase, not an invented access claim. Do not add $149-only Full buyers to Premium numerator. Count refunds/cancellations distinctly and allow adjusted net conversion reporting as a separate labeled view.

**Required secondary measurements:** assessment-start→completion, completion→first Free conversation, continued meaningful Free engagement, checkout initiation, verified Full purchase, verified Premium purchase, verified activation, permitted post-conversion retention at documented 7/30/90-day windows (only once the cohort is mature), feedback usefulness and actual Free value. Give numerator, denominator, definition, time window, source-quality/unknown count and suppression thresholds. Do not imply a 30-day cohort is mature before the window elapses. Protect small cells, no named sponsor/channel activity. Offer source-level aggregated Compass vs non-Compass channel performance only with lawful attribution and privacy safeguards. Conversion is a business metric, never the sole Steward quality objective.

## J-UNI-05 Campaign/QR/source
Opaque campaign codes and QR destination are independent of account profile, church, sponsor and commercial benefits. Redirect to canonical public assessment route; allow direct Free routing where participant explicitly chooses. Source capture is permitted only with consent/other approved legal basis and retention policy. Deletion/withdrawal invalidates eligible join and reporting derivatives. Unknown source stays `unknown`. No inference from IP, church name or ForgedLife membership. Never send a participant's CFM/Blueprint/Steward content to ForgedLife absent distinct authorized consent plus service checks.

## J-UNI-06 Security and availability
Actual public release is conditional on independently reviewed amendment to invitation-only adult Arizona-first pilot controls, demonstrated identity/eligibility, provider fit, privacy/legal review, operational staffing, rights clearance, test passes, staged rollback and explicit final owner release approval. An engineering-ready public platform is not permission to expose unreviewed functionality publicly. Public safety resources are not gated by signup.
