## Objective and acceptance boundary
Build one participant-facing Christian AI Steward to support useful conversation immediately, accurate assessment interpretation, one participant-owned evolving Blueprint, Living/Premium decision support, retreat and legacy across the intended full V2 product. The participant-facing AI must never be the authority for authentication, policy, access, payments, scoring, persistence or sending. Public production is separately gated.

## Product and experience model
Experience entry paths: public visitor may ask a useful question; direct account setup without assessment; Created for More-first results then protected save and continue; later Energy Audit; referral; sponsor/leader invitation. All converge on one canonical verified participant identity, preserving source and consent separately. Returning members resume existing approved context where authorized. Do not force an assessment, church, faith declaration or payment to receive Free help. Provide safety resources even when authentication fails.

Free Blueprint includes cover, My North Star, My Starting Plan / My Next Faithful Step. Full includes T4's exact thirteen ordered pages and the North Star subtitle. Free is genuinely helpful but bounded; retained Full content and history stay eligible for viewing/export after paid access ends; continuing guided Full redevelopment across sessions belongs to active Build/Premium or equivalent grant, not an unlimited Free substitute. No invented numerical quota. Qualified Full owner never automatically repurchases initial Full.

Conversation quality contract: adapt to participant intent and capacity; understand actual stated concern; compare relevant authorized evidence with chosen values/season and competing responsibilities; identify strengths worth protecting and possible misalignment without assuming failure; teach applicable Blueprint meaning and why; offer a proportionate optional next step and evidence of fit. Sometimes listening, doing nothing new, protecting existing habits, obtaining human input or waiting is the most faithful answer. One focused follow-up question only when it materially improves judgment. Use relevant Scripture references and original explanation, no restricted NIV passages or disguised close paraphrase. Do not make assessment grades spiritual judgments. Never claim revelation, a human relationship or licensed authority.

### Journeys to implement and test
1. **New assessment:** Complete current instrument; show dated section scores, completeness and pattern insight. For actual CFM4, seven layer results, no invented Pillar diagnostics; Energy Audit four category readings separately. Choose explore strength, explore concern or begin Free Blueprint; deep link email uses account-recovery and sensitive data limits. No duplicate account or forced retake.
2. **Free-to-Full:** Protected application offer from catalogue; explicit commercial choice; payment and pending entitlement verified; bundle explicitly activates at chosen time after confirmed payment; resumes existing context. Original wording is not approved by payment. If checkout fails/abandoned continue Free and retained access.
3. **Build:** AG02 displays exact first-person proposal/target/scope; participant approves exact displayed wording; application writes immutable version and receipt; field definition and section function evaluated separately; accepted text alone never proves saved. Exports reflect current actual state and accurate gaps.
4. **Living:** User-selectable Listen First, Think, Take a Step; Check Me and Decision Mode; optional routines and reviews; relevant context, capacity, Protect/Restore/Build/Multiply teaching, financial season and living Legacy. No implicit commitments, reminders or new season. Grace-centered Return recognizes cause and safe renewal, not condemnation.
5. **Year in Review / Annual Retreat:** Free guide accessible without a private personalized record. Premium guided digital experience selects approved records and actual history for reflection, shows explicit gaps, previews proposed next season / words and produces eligible export only after necessary choices and renderer confirmation. Changes to canonical Blueprint remain separate approval and persistence actions. Print path disabled until reviewed.
6. **Organization and ForgedLife:** Identity for organization admins/facilitators validated by server-side role assignment. Participant opts in to any optional data sharing with exact recipient/scope/purpose. Cohort/sponsor eligibility grants do not imply disclosure. Opaque signed/replayed-safe integration events use allowlisted fields and reconciliation. No cross-product shared profile by default.
7. **Re-engagement / Legacy:** Welcome with appropriate choice and currently authorized selected context; absence is not drift. Invite living generosity through modeling/teaching/equipping willing people. An invitation/referral never establishes Legacy completion.
