<a id="PD-SEASON-B0009"></a>

### 3. Key definitions

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0020"></a>
Current Life Season. A meaningful period in which a particular set of relationships, responsibilities, opportunities, constraints, transitions, and assignments shape what faithfulness requires now.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0021"></a>
Blueprint-area posture. The current assignment of a Blueprint area, such as Restore, Protect, Deepen, Build, Advance, Govern, Launch, Explore, Release, Transfer, or Multiply.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0022"></a>
90-Day Focus. The few concrete outcomes receiving concentrated attention within the larger season. A 90-day focus is not the same as the season.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0023"></a>
For the Right Season item. A meaningful goal, idea, dream, or opportunity intentionally deferred without being discarded.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0024"></a>
Evidence of Alignment. Observable evidence that the participant’s lived priorities are matching the approved Blueprint; the measure may be a count, yes/no, rating, action, story, or milestone.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0025"></a>
Transition. A meaningful change that may alter the participant’s responsibilities, rhythms, identity expression, relationships, capacity, or calling in the next season.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0026"></a>
Capacity Zone. A reflective coaching description of present capacity: Depleted → Restore; Constrained → Simplify; Healthy → Build; Strong → Stretch. It is not a diagnosis.

### 7. Approved season logic and coaching safeguards

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0097"></a>
#### Season discernment sequence

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0098"></a>
1.  Name current reality: relationships, responsibilities, opportunities, constraints, transitions, and assignments.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0099"></a>
2.  Review Bedrock, Foundation, values, Current Calling, and existing commitments.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0100"></a>
3.  Assess current capacity and Energy without assuming that busyness equals depletion.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0101"></a>
4.  Identify what should Restore, Protect, Deepen, Build, Advance, Govern, Release, Transfer, or Multiply.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0102"></a>
5.  Right-size the season: determine whether it needs more focus, more courage, or both.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0103"></a>
6.  Move meaningful but mistimed work into For the Right Season with context and an optional trigger.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0104"></a>
7.  Define Evidence of Alignment, completion signals, and transition signals.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0105"></a>
8.  Review periodically without forcing artificial expiration.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0106"></a>
#### Season Overload — possible signals

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0107"></a>
Too many Build Now priorities.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0108"></a>
New commitments added without anything leaving.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0109"></a>
Protect areas losing time, energy, or relational presence.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0110"></a>
Chronic context switching, unfinished commitments, or reclaimed delegated work.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0111"></a>
Declining Energy or insufficient support capacity.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0112"></a>
#### Season Underreach — possible signals

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0113"></a>
Healthy margin and capacity with little meaningful investment.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0114"></a>
Calling or important action repeatedly postponed without new evidence.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0115"></a>
Fear or comfort consistently described as discernment or protection.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0116"></a>
Capabilities, relationships, or resources left unused despite a recurring sense of responsibility.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0117"></a>
Conversation safeguard. Signals are questions, not verdicts. Steward should help the participant see and name the pattern. Use language such as “Does that fit your experience?” and “Can I challenge you on a tension I am hearing?”

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0118"></a>
### 8. Transition pathway

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0119"></a>
1.  Remember — What just happened?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0120"></a>
2.  Celebrate and/or Grieve — What deserves to be honored or mourned?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0121"></a>
3.  Reflect — What did the last season teach about God, self, people, capacity, and calling?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0122"></a>
4.  Harvest — What wisdom, relationships, rhythms, Guardrails, stories, and strengths should come forward?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0123"></a>
5.  Release — What roles, expectations, goals, burdens, or identity attachments belonged to the prior season?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0124"></a>
6.  Retrieve Right Season Items — What was intentionally saved?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0125"></a>
7.  Discern — What might God be inviting the participant to notice, receive, release, or do?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0126"></a>
8.  Envision — Who is the participant becoming, and what could faithfulness look like?

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0127"></a>
9.  Name the New Season.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0128"></a>
10.  Update the Blueprint and establish participant-approved Evidence of Alignment.

<a id="PD-SEASON-B0129"></a>
11.  Preserve the prior Blueprint and season context subject to approved retention and deletion rules.
