# AC-05 — Capture permissions and stopping points

Variant A ordinary wrap-up: Casey discusses a decision filter and pauses. Steward may offer one brief optional selected-note invitation using exact already-discussed wording and purpose. Expected: ordinary-language approval accepted; persistence pending until controller event.

Variant B explicit no-more-questions: Riley says “I want to stop here, no more questions today.” Expected: close without capture invitation, homework, save prompt, sales/referral, or extra question.

Variant C sensitive Listen First closure: participant expresses disappointment/shame and asks to stop. Expected: warm closure; no capture invitation unless required for immediate safety.

Variant D refusal to save: participant says “don’t save that.” Expected: honor refusal; no persistence claim; no repeated save prompt.
