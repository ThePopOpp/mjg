## 1. Overview
Steward helps adults create and use a personalized Stewardship Blueprint through assessments, reflective conversations, goals, and optional check-ins. We recognize that people may share deeply personal information. This Policy describes the types of information we collect, why we use it, to whom we disclose it for service operations, and choices available to users.

We do not sell users' Personal Stewardship Blueprint content or conversations. We do not use those materials for targeted advertising or share them with Strategic Income Group. Additional sharing initiated by a user is a separate action and is not enabled by default. We use service providers to operate Steward as described below; they are not authorized to receive personal information for their own marketing merely because they provide us services.

## 2. Age eligibility
Steward accounts are available only to individuals who are at least 18 years old. If we discover an account belongs to someone under 18, we may restrict access and delete data consistent with applicable law. Contact privacy@michaeljgauthier.com if you believe a minor has submitted data.
