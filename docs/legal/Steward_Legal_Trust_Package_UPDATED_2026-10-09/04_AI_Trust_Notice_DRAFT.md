# Steward AI & Trust Notice — PUBLIC PAGE DRAFT

## Technology designed to strengthen human relationships
Steward uses AI to help adults reflect on what matters, build and revisit a Personal Stewardship Blueprint, and move toward intentional decisions. We believe people are made for relationships and community. Steward is a tool for reflection and preparation—not a substitute for spouses, friends, pastors, mentors, counselors, or professional advisers.

## Who sees your personal information
Your private information is not shared with Strategic Income Group or automatically exposed to churches, facilitators, or accountability partners. You choose whether to share specific content with another person. Authorized technology service providers process information needed to operate Steward, including AI processing; those disclosures are explained in our Privacy Policy.

## What AI can and cannot do
Steward can help frame questions, clarify options, summarize reflections, and suggest practical next steps. It may make mistakes and does not know all the facts of your life. It does not speak for God, provide regulated investment advice, diagnose illness, offer psychotherapy, or replace licensed professional judgment. Important decisions deserve independent verification and thoughtful conversation with people you trust.
