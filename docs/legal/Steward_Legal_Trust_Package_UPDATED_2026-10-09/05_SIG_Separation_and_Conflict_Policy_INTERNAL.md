## Purpose
Acknowledge shared ownership or management while preserving genuine operational, regulatory, and data separation. The shared office address is administratively permissible only with appropriate operational controls. This policy does not replace SIG's ADV, Code of Ethics, Regulation S-P program, records policies, or other advisory compliance requirements.

## Mandatory controls
1. **Separate systems and identities:** Unique company accounts, access controls, API credentials, billing, payment processing, file stores, analytics, CRM pipelines, and permissions. No SIG planner or employee gets access to Steward user content by SIG role. No Steward worker gets access to SIG client records by Steward role.
2. **No cross-company data transfers:** No imports/exports, data syncs, mailing-list matching, lead tracking, lookalike audiences, or shared data warehouse connecting customers across businesses. No SIG contact lists uploaded into Steward and no Steward lists uploaded to SIG.
3. **No referrals or economic transfers based on introductions:** No finder fees, referral commissions, revenue shares, preferential discounts or other referral-based compensation between entities. Audit cross-promotion and benefits of any shared staff or marketing effort, including non-cash benefits.
4. **No embedded investment advice:** Steward's AI and user experience must not recommend specific SIG securities models, client accounts, trades, or adviser assignments. If user asks for a financial professional, use a neutral recommendation to consult a qualified professional of the user's choice, not SIG-specific routing.
5. **No implied endorsement:** SIG branding, testimonial content, SEC advisory claims, AUM, SIG account outcomes, or office signage must not cause users to think SIG provides/guarantees Steward. No representation that Steward is SEC-regulated through SIG.
6. **Physical separation:** A shared postal address does not confer access. Use segregated mail, locked storage, restricted staff access, and independent contact channels. If shared administrative staff, document duties, confidentiality training, and system permissions.
7. **Cost allocations:** Shared rent, facilities, or administrative resources, if any, should be documented, reasonably allocated, and reviewed with legal, accounting, and SIG compliance. No arrangement should quietly function as referral compensation.
8. **Incident boundaries:** If a suspected incident touches either business, investigate impact independently, coordinate only as legally appropriate, and document both entities' notification obligations.
9. **Review every integration or marketing change:** CCO written review before a new partner link, reciprocal referral, shared employee access, data export, cross-domain tracking, church-group reports, or common CRM integration.
10. **Regulatory records:** SIG CCO to document outside business activities/conflicts, appropriate ADV disclosures, books-and-records consequences, and Marketing Rule implications. SIG remains responsible for its own Regulation S-P safeguards.

## Escalation
Any exception requires prior written approval from Steward management, legal counsel, and SIG CCO, with regulatory and user-consent analysis. Do not treat a user's ability to share a document independently as permission for a system integration.

## Contact-channel segregation
The Steward email aliases at michaeljgauthier.com must route only to authorized Steward personnel, not to SIG shared support mailboxes or SIG CRM/ticketing systems. Use separate access controls, logs, and retention settings. Verify MX/mail administration and any shared back-office personnel do not expose Steward correspondence to SIG systems. Although both brands may use the same street address, written correspondence and electronic requests must remain segregated.
