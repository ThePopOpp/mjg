How to Use This Series
This automation is written for a participant after he has accepted the invitation. It assumes he is part of a live group, cohort, church, or men's challenge. The copy can be adapted for a fully digital challenge by changing meeting language to cohort, group call, or app check-in.
The cadence intentionally uses more than one touchpoint per week, but each email has a single job: prepare, prompt action, gather feedback, deepen community, or help the participant multiply the experience. The tone is pastoral, encouraging, direct, and practical.
* Use the Weekly Launch emails three to five days before each session, especially if participants need pre-work time.
* Use the Action Follow-Up emails within 24 hours after each session to keep the transformation moving between meetings.
* Use the simple reminders only when needed; avoid overwhelming men who are already engaging.
* Keep reply-to personal. The phrase “I want to hear from you” is part of the ministry posture, not merely a feedback tactic.
* For every public story, testimonial, quote, or example, ask separately and get explicit permission after the emotional moment has passed.
Source-Grounded Strategy
This series is designed around the Stewardship Blueprint ecosystem: The Life You're Building is the public invitation and teaching narrative, Stewardship Blueprint is the framework, Created for More Check-In is the front-door assessment, and ForgedLife supports accountability, habits, challenges, prayer, and group follow-through.
The emails emphasize the seven layers of the Blueprint: Bedrock, Foundation, Four Pillars, Guardrails, Keystone Habits, Energy, and Legacy. They also keep the movement from becoming information-only by requiring one faithful step, one conversation, and one rhythm at multiple points in the challenge.

Implementation Notes for the Email Platform
1. Build the sequence relative to the group start date rather than calendar dates so future cohorts can reuse it.
2. Create a “Meeting Day” field and schedule weekly launch emails three to five days before the group session.
3. Use conditional content for book waitlist and ForgedLife CTAs so participants are not asked to join lists they have already joined.
4. Route replies to a real inbox that Michael or a group leader checks. The relational invitation is central to the strategy.
5. After Week 3 and Week 6, capture feedback both through forms and replies. Forms create data; replies create relationship.
6. Track opens and clicks, but the more important outcomes are replies, completed Check-Ins, actions named, conversations started, guardrails built, habits tested, and 30-day durability.
7. After the 30-day follow-up, segment men into next pathways: personal plan, continue group, lead/host, book updates, ForgedLife, church/ministry interest, or invite another man.
