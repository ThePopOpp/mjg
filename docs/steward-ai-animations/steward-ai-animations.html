A Blueprint for What Matters Most

The Stewardship Blueprint helps individuals and families reflect on the areas of life that should not be managed separately: faith, family, finances, future, and legacy.

01

Faith

Reflect on the beliefs and values that shape how you define stewardship and success.

02

Family

Consider the people you are called to love, lead, protect, support, and serve.

03

Finances

Organize the resources, responsibilities, and decisions that affect your financial life.

04

Future

Think intentionally about the next season of life and what needs to be prepared.

05

Legacy

Explore the impact, generosity, wisdom, and values you want to pass on to others.

How It Works

The experience is designed to help users move from reflection to clarity through a simple guided process.

1

Sign In or Request Access

Enter the private MJG WebApp through a secure, guided experience.

2

Complete Guided Reflections

Answer thoughtful prompts around faith, family, finances, future, giving, and legacy.

3

Organize Key Information

Complete forms, review resources, and prepare for a meaningful stewardship conversation.

4

Get Help From Steward

Steward, the MJG AI Agent, helps users understand the process and know what to do next.

5

Meet With the MJG Team

Come prepared for a deeper conversation with clarity, context, and direction.
