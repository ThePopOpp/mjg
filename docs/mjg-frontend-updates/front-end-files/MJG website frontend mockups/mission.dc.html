✦ Mission & Vision

Using God-Given Resources for God-Given Purposes.

My mission is to encourage and inspire people to use their God-given resources for God-given purposes.

Those resources include far more than money. They include time, relationships, health, talents, attention, opportunity, energy, influence, and everything else we have been entrusted to steward.

Explore the Stewardship Blueprint

Why It Exists

A Better Way to Measure a Meaningful Life.

Our culture offers visible scoreboards: income, career growth, possessions, recognition, influence, and busyness. Some of those things may be good, but none is big enough to tell us what our lives are for.

The Stewardship Blueprint exists to help people move from drift toward faithful living by connecting belief to daily choices and long-term direction.

Our Vision

Impact Worth Multiplying.

I hope the Stewardship Blueprint encourages tens of thousands of people to live more intentionally and multiply generosity and faithful influence through their families, churches, communities, workplaces, and relationships.

The goal is not to measure a person’s worth by a score, gift amount, follower count, or platform adoption. It is to encourage faithful action and let impact grow through people.
