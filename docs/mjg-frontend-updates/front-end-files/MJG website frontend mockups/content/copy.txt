What Kind of Life Are You Actually Building?
Most people don’t intentionally drift. Life simply gets full.
We work hard, care for people, pursue opportunities, and manage countless responsibilities. But somewhere along
the way, we can stop asking whether the life we’re building reflects what matters most.
Your life is taking shape every day—through your calendar, relationships, habits, health, decisions, and use of

The Stewardship Blueprint is a whole-life framework that helps you stop drifting, steward what God has entrusted to
you, and build a life that matters.

HERO
One Framework. Your Whole Life.
You are building a life, whether you have designed it or not. The Stewardship Blueprint helps you see the entire
structure and bring your daily choices into alignment with what matters most.
It begins with an important belief: our lives are not merely ours to use. They are entrusted to us to steward faithfully.
CALLS TO ACTION: Take the Free Check-In → /created-for-more-check-in
IMAGE / VIDEO: Media: Full original architectural Blueprint as hero or immediately below text.
THE BUILDING
See How the Layers Work Together.
BEDROCK — FAITH & IDENTITY. Your identity is deeper than what you accomplish, own, or produce. Faith is not just
one room in the house; it shapes the entire structure.
FOUNDATION — MISSION, VALUES, CALLING & PURPOSE. What matters most to you? What does faithful living look
like in this season? Foundation translates conviction into direction.
FOUR PILLARS — FAMILY, FITNESS, FUN & FINANCES. Relationships, health, joy, and financial resources are
connected. Strengthening one while neglecting the others eventually affects the whole life.
GUARDRAILS — BOUNDARIES THAT PROTECT. Pre-decided limits protect your priorities, integrity, relationships, and
freedom before pressure creates regret.
KEYSTONE HABITS — RHYTHMS THAT STRENGTHEN. Small, repeatable actions shape the life you live more than
occasional bursts of motivation.
ENERGY — CAPACITY TO LIVE IT. Physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual energy must be received, invested,
renewed, and protected.
LEGACY — WHAT YOU MULTIPLY WHILE LIVING. Your legacy takes shape in the people you influence, equip, serve,
and encourage now. Generosity grows when others learn to practice and pass it on. Estate arrangements may support
continuity but do not define Legacy Giving.
CALLS TO ACTION: See the Assessments → /assessments • Explore Studies → /studies
IMAGE / VIDEO: Media: One separate crop/highlight for each layer if original vector files are available. Accessible text labels required.
HOW TO USE IT
Your Blueprint Changes With Your Season.
The Stewardship Blueprint is not a demand for perfect balance. Some seasons require more attention to family, others
to health, resources, service, or rest. The point is to notice what is truly entrusted to you and avoid leaving important
areas unattended indefinitely.
Begin by examining what is true now. Then choose one important next step. As your season changes, return to the
framework and refine the decisions you are making.

WHAT IT INCLUDES
A Living Guide, Not Just Another Document.
Your Blueprint can bring together your faith and identity; your mission, values, and purpose; the person you are
becoming; Family, Fitness, Fun, and Finances; financial direction; guardrails; drift signals; accountability; keystone
habits; energy; and the legacy your life is multiplying through others.
The standard Personal Blueprint structure includes a cover and twelve supporting pages. Each section helps answer a
practical question: What should I remember, protect, decide, practice, or revisit?
It should be personal enough to recognize yourself and usable enough to open on a difficult Tuesday.
CALLS TO ACTION: Explore the Framework → /stewardship-blueprint
IMAGE / VIDEO: Media: Page-turn gallery using 3–5 sanitized sample pages from the thirteen-page template.
BUILDING PROCESS
Notice. Clarify. Build. Live. Revisit.
START WITH AWARENESS — Take the Created for More Check-In or reflect on a current area of drift.
CLARIFY WHAT MATTERS — Identify the convictions, people, responsibilities, and priorities that need to guide this
season.
BUILD THE PLAN — Capture meaningful statements, boundaries, habits, and next steps rather than generic
aspirations.
PUT IT INTO LIFE — Let your calendar, conversations, and decisions reflect what you have written.
REVISIT & REFINE — Your identity and enduring mission may remain steady while your calling, roles, and
commitments change.
