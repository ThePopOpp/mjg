✦ About Michael

I’m Still Building, Too.

I’m Michael J. Gauthier. I’m a husband, father, follower of Jesus, financial planner, business leader, and someone who is still learning to live what matters most.

The Stewardship Blueprint is not a theory I developed from a distance. It grew out of my own questions, successes, misplaced scoreboards, relationships, faith journey, and ongoing desire to steward the life God has entrusted to me.

The Question

If Everything I’m Chasing Works, Will It Actually Matter?

Early in my career, I was ambitious. I worked hard and measured success through production, growth, titles, and recognition. None of those pursuits were inherently wrong. But they were incomplete.

When Kristi and I welcomed our first child, Colton, I started thinking about a different kind of success. He would not just need provision. He would watch what I valued, how I treated his mother, where I spent my time, and what I celebrated.

Later, trusted friends invited our family into a small-group Bible study. My faith began to move from a compartment of life toward the foundation of how I understood marriage, work, finances, service, and purpose.

The Journey

From Planning Money to Examining the Whole Life.

I founded Strategic Income Group in 2013 after leaving a large Wall Street firm. Financial planning taught me important lessons about discipline, responsibility, and wise use of resources.

It also revealed a deeper question: What is the money for? Many people have detailed financial plans without a clear picture of the life those resources are meant to serve.

That observation, together with my personal faith and family journey, helped shape the Stewardship Blueprint. It is an invitation to look beyond one scoreboard and steward the whole life.
