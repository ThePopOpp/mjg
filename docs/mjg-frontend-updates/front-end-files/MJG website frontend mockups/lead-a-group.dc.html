✦ Lead a Group

You Don’t Have to Be the Expert in the Room.

Great group conversations often begin with one person willing to create space for honesty, Scripture, reflection, and practical action.

Leading a Stewardship Blueprint study is not about giving six polished speeches. It is about serving people well and helping them move from insight to one meaningful next step.

Explore the Current Challenge Register as Facilitator

What Leaders Do

Create Space. Ask Good Questions. Encourage Action.

Prepare with the participant materials, welcome the group, maintain confidentiality, guide discussion rather than dominate it, and invite each person to choose a manageable commitment.

Good leaders listen carefully, respect the right to pass, avoid acting as another person’s therapist or professional advisor, and know when to involve appropriate church or professional support.
