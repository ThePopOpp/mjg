The Life You’re Building

How to Stop Drifting and Design a Life That Matters

What if you are working hard, achieving meaningful goals, and doing what responsible people do—but you have not stopped to ask whether you are building the right life?

This book invites you to look more closely at the life taking shape and discover what faithful, intentional stewardship can look like across every area of life.

Inside the Book

A Journey from Drift to Design.

The Life You’re Building begins with a wake-up call, introduces the Stewardship Blueprint, and helps readers evaluate relationships, health, joy, and finances; establish guardrails and habits; renew energy; rethink financial resources; and consider what their lives are multiplying through other people.

Each chapter combines story, Christian faith, questions, and practical next steps. The book is written for ordinary people with full calendars—not only financial professionals or seasoned church leaders.

Author Note

Why I Wrote It.

For a long time I measured progress through achievement and ambition. The birth of my first child prompted a different question: If everything I am chasing works, will it actually matter?

The answer did not arrive in a day. It became a long journey of learning to see faith, relationships, purpose, money, time, and influence as connected responsibilities.

I wrote this book to share the framework that continues to shape the life I am building.
