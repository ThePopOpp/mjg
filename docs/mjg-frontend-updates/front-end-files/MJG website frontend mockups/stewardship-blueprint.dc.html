How to Use It

Your Blueprint Changes With Your Season.

The Stewardship Blueprint is not a demand for perfect balance. Some seasons require more attention to family, others to health, resources, service, or rest. The point is to notice what is truly entrusted to you and avoid leaving important areas unattended indefinitely.

Begin by examining what is true now. Then choose one important next step. As your season changes, return to the framework and refine the decisions you are making.

Faith & Welcome

Rooted in Faith. Open to Honest Questions.

The framework reflects a Christian understanding of life as a gift entrusted by God. You do not need to have everything figured out—or every belief settled—to begin reflecting on the kind of life you are building.
